## About the Project

**Career Progression & Transition System** is a software project designed to support professionals who are considering a **career transition, professional relocation or repositioning in the job market**.

The system was developed with the goal of helping users reflect on their current professional profile, evaluate possible career directions and identify potential paths for professional development.

Instead of focusing exclusively on job listings, the project approaches career transition as a structured decision-making process.

```text
Current Professional Profile
            │
            ▼
    Career Assessment
            │
            ▼
   Skills & Experience
            │
            ▼
     Career Options
            │
            ▼
   Transition Planning
            │
            ▼
    Professional Growth
```

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Professional relocation is often more complex than simply finding another job.

A professional considering a career change may need to answer questions such as:

* What are my current professional skills?
* Which areas are compatible with my experience?
* What skills should I develop?
* Which career paths are realistic?
* What gaps exist between my current profile and my desired position?
* How can I prepare for a transition?
* What should be my next professional step?

The project was conceived to explore how software can help organize these questions and support a more structured career-planning process.
